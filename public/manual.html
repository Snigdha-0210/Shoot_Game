SECTION 2: ON-RAIL COMBAT & 3-BUTTON CIPHER OVERRIDES [C, V, SPACE]

  • Lock-On Engagement: Operative moves on-rail and automatically locks onto 2 to 3 distant, scattered hostiles per sector spaced across long corridor sprints.
  • Strict Engagement Stop: Movement stops when an enemy is targeted. You cannot advance until the hostile is neutralized.
  • 3-Button Hardware Combat System: When locked onto an enemy, a randomized sequence is generated strictly from 3 buttons:
    • Allowed Inputs: [C: Circuit C], [V: Circuit V], and [SPACE: Kinetic Trigger] (or click the on-screen badges).
  • Tightening Reaction Windows: Input the correct sequence before the countdown expires:
    • Sector 01: 1–2 buttons • 3.0s ➔ 2.6s ➔ 2.4s reaction window
    • Sector 02: 2 buttons • 2.4s ➔ 2.2s ➔ 2.0s reaction window
    • Sector 03: 2–3 buttons • 2.0s ➔ 1.8s ➔ 1.7s reaction window
    • Sector 04: 3 buttons (Guards) / 4 buttons (General Malikov Boss) • 1.8s ➔ 1.6s ➔ 2.4s (Boss Showdown!)
  • Wrong Key Penalty: Entering an incorrect cipher plays an error buzzer and subtracts -0.25s from the reaction timer.
  • Timeout Failure: If the countdown timer expires, the hostile fires a fatal shot for instant mission failure!
  • Global Timer: The 4-sector campaign runs on a single continuous 2-minute (120s) countdown clock.
